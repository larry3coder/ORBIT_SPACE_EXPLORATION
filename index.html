Humanity continues to push the boundaries of exploration.

Artemis Program

2022 — Present

NASA's Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon for the first time since Apollo 17 in 1972, including the first woman and first person of color to walk on the lunar surface.

Explore Mission →
NASA / JPLActive

Mars Perseverance Rover

Landed Feb 18, 2021

Perseverance explores Jezero Crater, searching for signs of ancient microbial life, collecting rock samples for future Earth return, and testing oxygen production from the Martian atmosphere.

Explore Mission →
NASA / ESA / CSAActive

James Webb Space Telescope

Launched Dec 25, 2021

The most powerful space telescope ever built, JWST observes the universe in infrared — revealing the earliest galaxies, exoplanet atmospheres, and star-forming regions with unprecedented clarity.

Explore Mission →
NASA / JPLInterstellar

Voyager 1 & 2

Launched 1977 — Still Transmitting

The farthest human-made objects from Earth. Voyager 1 is over 24 billion km from the Sun and has crossed into interstellar space, still sending data back to Earth across the void.

Explore Mission →
NASA / JPLEn Route

Europa Clipper

Launched Oct 14, 2024

Europa Clipper will perform 49 flybys of Jupiter's moon Europa, investigating whether its subsurface ocean could support life. Jupiter arrival expected in 2030.
